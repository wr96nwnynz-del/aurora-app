Features

Everything you need to
build a lasting habit

Aurora combines science-backed motivation with social accountability to make morning walks stick.

🔥

Walk Streaks

Track your consecutive morning walks and watch your streak grow. Missing a day breaks it — which is exactly the motivation you need.

🤝

Accountability Partners

Add a friend or family member as your walking buddy. They'll get a notification if you skip a morning — and you'll get one if they do.

🗺️

Route Tracking

Record your walk, see your route on the map, and log distance. Your routes stay private on your device.

🏆

Leaderboard

Compete with friends on a private leaderboard. See who's walking the most and celebrate each other's progress.

🎁

Rewards

Earn points for every walk and redeem them for real discounts and offers from partner brands in the rewards catalog.

⏰

Morning Reminders

Set your target walk time and Aurora will remind you every morning — personalized to when you want to head out.

How it works

Simple by design

No complicated plans. Just walk in the morning, every day.

1

Set your walk time

Tell Aurora when you want to walk each morning and set your daily reminder.

2

Go for your walk

Open the app, start your walk, and let Aurora record your route and distance in the background.

3

Build your streak

Complete walks before your cutoff time each day to keep your streak alive and earn rewards points.

4

Stay accountable

Add a partner so you both hold each other to the habit — the social pressure is real, and it works.

A subscription that rewards you for showing up
